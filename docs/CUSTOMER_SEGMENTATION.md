# Customer Segmentation

## Overview

This analysis groups purchasing customers according to their behaviour using RFM analysis and K-Means clustering.

RFM represents:

- **Recency:** Days since the customer's latest purchase
- **Frequency:** Total orders placed by the customer
- **Monetary:** Total revenue generated by the customer

## Data Source

The analysis uses:

`silver_marketing_interactions`

Only successful purchases were included:

- `converted = 1`
- `orders = 1`

The final dataset contains:

- 155 purchasing customers
- 169 orders
- PKR 3,827,575 in revenue

## RFM Analysis

The latest purchase occurred on December 30, 2025. Therefore, December 31, 2025 was selected as the analysis date.

Customer-level RFM features were calculated as follows:

- Recency = Analysis date minus latest purchase date
- Frequency = Sum of orders
- Monetary = Sum of revenue

## Feature Preparation

Frequency and Monetary values were positively skewed. Logarithmic transformations were applied to reduce the influence of extreme values:

- `log_frequency = log(1 + frequency)`
- `log_monetary = log(1 + monetary)`

The clustering features were:

- Recency
- Log Frequency
- Log Monetary

The features were standardized using `StandardScaler` so that each feature had a comparable influence on K-Means.

## Cluster Selection

K-Means was evaluated using values of `k` from 2 to 6.

| k | Silhouette Score | Cluster Sizes |
|---:|---:|---|
| 2 | 0.7644 | 13, 142 |
| 3 | 0.5161 | 72, 70, 13 |
| 4 | 0.6448 | 13, 63, 51, 28 |
| 5 | 0.6297 | 53, 13, 15, 21, 53 |
| 6 | 0.5668 | 35, 21, 45, 13, 10, 31 |

Although `k = 2` produced the highest silhouette score, it created only two broad customer groups. A final value of `k = 4` was selected because it provided good separation, reasonable cluster sizes, and more useful marketing segments.

## Final Customer Segments

| Segment | Customers | Average Recency | Average Frequency | Average Monetary | Total Revenue | Revenue Share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Loyal Customers | 13 | 149.54 | 2.08 | PKR 38,057.69 | PKR 494,750 | 12.93% |
| Recent Low-Value Customers | 63 | 110.19 | 1.00 | PKR 5,894.05 | PKR 371,325 | 9.70% |
| At-Risk Customers | 51 | 287.22 | 1.00 | PKR 10,862.75 | PKR 554,000 | 14.47% |
| High-Value One-Time Customers | 28 | 138.93 | 1.00 | PKR 85,982.14 | PKR 2,407,500 | 62.90% |

## Marketing Recommendations

### Loyal Customers

Use loyalty rewards, referral incentives, and personalized repeat-purchase campaigns.

### Recent Low-Value Customers

Use welcome offers, relevant product recommendations, and low-cost automated campaigns.

### At-Risk Customers

Use re-engagement messages, limited-time discounts, and win-back campaigns.

### High-Value One-Time Customers

Use VIP offers, premium product recommendations, and incentives encouraging a second purchase.

## Business Findings

- Most purchasing customers placed only one order.
- Only 13 customers made repeat purchases.
- High-Value One-Time Customers represent 18.06% of customers but generate 62.90% of total revenue.
- At-Risk Customers have an average Recency of 287.22 days and require re-engagement.
- Encouraging high-value customers to make a second purchase is a major business opportunity.

## Output

`gold_customer_segments`

The table contains:

- `customer_id`

- `recency`
- `frequency`
- `monetary`
- `cluster`
- `segment_name`
- `recommended_action`

## Limitation

Purchase-based RFM includes only customers who placed at least one order. Customers with no purchases are excluded and should be analysed separately as non-converting prospects.
